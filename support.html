Frequently asked questions

How do I play?
Tap "Local" on the main menu, then "Start game". Move with the pad on the left and place bombs with the button on the right. Stay out of the blast and be the last one standing.
What do the glowing cards do?
Violet with a bomb: carry one more bomb at a time. Orange with a flame: blasts reach further. Teal with a lightning bolt: move faster.
Can several people play on one device?
Yes, with game controllers or a hardware keyboard. Connect them, then add local players on the room screen.
How do I change my character or the arena?
On the room screen, tap the portrait with a red ring to switch character, and tap an arena thumbnail to choose where to play.
Does the game need the internet?
No.

怎么玩？
在主菜单点"本机对战"，再点"开始游戏"。左侧方向盘移动，右侧按钮放炸弹。躲开爆炸，活到最后。
发光的卡片是什么？
紫色炸弹：多带一颗炸弹。橙色火焰：爆炸范围更远。青色闪电：移动更快。
可以多人在同一台设备上玩吗？
可以，需要游戏手柄或外接键盘。连接后在房间界面添加本机玩家。
需要联网吗？
不需要。

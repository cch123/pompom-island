Pom Pom Island — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Summary

Pom Pom Island does not collect, store on any server, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your device

The app saves two settings on your device so they are still there next time: the nickname you type on the main menu, and whether sound is on. It also keeps a short technical log (device model, system version and what the app was doing) to help diagnose problems. None of this is sent anywhere by the app. Deleting the app deletes it.

Network

The game is played on one device and works without a network connection.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

砰砰小岛 — 隐私政策

《砰砰小岛》不收集、不上传、不出售、不分享任何个人数据。没有账号，没有广告，没有统计分析。

App 只在你的设备上保存两项设置：主菜单里填写的昵称，以及声音开关。此外会在本机保留一份简短的技术日志（设备型号、系统版本、App 当时在做什么），用于排查问题。这些内容都不会被 App 发送到任何地方，删除 App 即同时删除。

游戏在单台设备上进行，无需联网。
